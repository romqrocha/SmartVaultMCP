# Cybersecurity case
- Question: "Should exploits be publicly shared?"
- Main thesis
	- Yes, exploits should be publicly shared
- Arguments:
	- Users should have the right to control their own data
	- When security vulnerabilities are exploited, Companies lose control of users data
	- When a company loses control of the data, users cannot control their data
	- When contacted privately, many companies do not act to solve the vulnerabilities
