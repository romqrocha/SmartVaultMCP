- Objectivists do not make claims to right and wrong, they just claim there is at least 1 absolute moral value that is shared
# Metaethics
- The questions of what kinds of things exist in ethics
- Can we know if something is morally true?
- How can we imply something is morally true?
# Normative Ethics
- The "tools" used to discuss ethics
# Applied Ethics
- Applying ethical theories to real-world scenarios
# Absolutism
- Always true or false; there are no exceptions
# Skepticism
- Skeptical of the ability to find/know moral truths, rather than skeptical of the *existence* of absolute moral truths.
# Moral Subjectivism
- Right and wrong are defined by the subjects personal beliefs
	- A sample size of 1
- A type of Relativist
