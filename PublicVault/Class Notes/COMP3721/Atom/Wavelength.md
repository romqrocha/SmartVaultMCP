# Wavelength
- The distance a signal can travel in one cycle
- Usually measured in micrometers(um)
- Depends on the propagation speed of the medium
- Shown using Lambda($\lambda$)
## Frequency vs Wavelength
- [[Frequency]] is independent of the transmission medium
	- It is simply a "perfect" calculated signal
- Wavelength relies on both the *frequency* and *transmission medium*
`C is the propagation speed of the medium`
$\lambda = c/f$
