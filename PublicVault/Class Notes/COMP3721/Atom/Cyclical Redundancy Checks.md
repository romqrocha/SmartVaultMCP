- Used in LAN and WAN
- Uses a division to generate extra bits to error checking
![[DataComm_CRC_Sizes.png]]
- $k$ is the size of the dataword
- *n* is the size of the codeword
- The **Divisor** is decided and agreed on by sender and receiver
![[DataComm_CRC_Encoding.png]]
- If you divide the codeword by the divisor, you should end up with 0
