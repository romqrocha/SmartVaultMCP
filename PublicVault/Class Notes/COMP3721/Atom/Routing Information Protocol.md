- Based on [[Distance Vector Routing]]
- Routers in a RIP network advertise the cost of reaching different networks instead of reaching other nodes
- Normally used in small [[Autonomous System|AS]]'s
- Cost is defined as number of hops
	- Max cost of a math can be 15
	- 16 is counted as Infinity/No Connection
	- This means RIP can't be used in AS's with a diameter of 16+
