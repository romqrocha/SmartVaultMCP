- Connects [[Network]] to other networks
	- This means its an inter-networking device
- Level 3 device
	- Implements [[Physical Layer]]
	- Implements [[Data-link Layer]]
	- Implements [[Network Layer]]
- Does not keep information about the connection state
- Treats each packet as an independent entity
- Has a [[MAC address]] as well as an [[IP Address]] for each of its interfaces
	- Because each link may have their own Data-Link or Physical protocol
- Modifies the Source and Destination Link-Layer(MAC) addresses of the packet before forwarding the packet based on the [[Routing and Forwarding#Routing Table|Routing Table]]
