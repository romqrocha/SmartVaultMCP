- Within the [[Network Layer]]
- A [[Message]] sent from an end system to another is divided into [[Packets]]
- No resource reservation
	- Resources are allocated on demand
	- There is no reserved bandwidth on the link
	- There is no scheduled processing time for each packet
- One subcategory: [[Datagram Network]]
- Packets may travel different paths to reach their destination
	- This means there is a non-uniform delay for packets of a message
	- also a possibility of out-of-order/lost/dropped packets
		- This is handled by upper-layer protocols(usually [[Transport Layer]])
- No setup or teardown phases for routing/switching
- The Destination Addresses([[IP Address]]) and corresponding forwarding output ports are recorded in the Routing/Forwarding table of the router
