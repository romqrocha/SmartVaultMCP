# #week/1 
## #fc #definition Communication
- The exchange of Information

## #fc #definition Telecommunication
- Communication from a distance
	- Telephone
	- Telegraph
	- Television
	- etc.

## #fc #definition Data
- Raw facts or information
	- text
	- numbers
	- images
	- audio
	- video
- Must be transmitted/presented in a form agreed on by sender and receiver

## #fc #definition Data Communication
- The exchange of data between two devices via a transmission medium

## #fc #definition Digital Communication
- All digital messages should be represented and transmitted as bits

## #fc #definition Analog Communication
- Transmission of information using **continuous signals** that vary in amplitude, frequency, or phase

## #fc #definition Data Communication System
- A network of hard- & soft-ware that enables the exchange of data between devices

## #fc What mediums do we have?
- Fiber Optic, carries light
- Air/Space, carries Radio Waves
- Copper, carries Electricity (Electric Conductive Material)

## #fc How do we measure effectiveness of Communication?
- Delivery
	- Data goes to the correct destination
- Accuracy
	- Delivers the data correctly
- Timeliness
	- Delivers the data in a timely manner (effective!)
- Jitter
	- Arrives with an even delay.
	- Uneven delay causes low-quality audio or video

## #fc What are the components of a Communication System?
- Protocol
	- The rules that the Sender and Receiver agree on
- Sender
- Receiver
- Message
	- The data being sent
- Medium
	- How the data is being sent

## #fc What are the types of data flow?
- Simplex
- Half-Duplex
- Full-Duplex

## #fc #definition Simplex
- One way data flow.
- Sender can only send, receiver can only receive.

## #fc #definition Half-Duplex
- Each device can send or receive, but only one can send at a time

## #fc #definition Full-Duplex
- All devices can send and receive at the same time.

## #fc #definition Network
- A system or structure of interconnected nodes or entities using some sort of link

## #fc #definition Nodes
- Individual entities or points, like train stations

## #fc #definition Edges
- Links or connections, defines **how** the nodes are connected
- Represent the relationships between nodes, like rails between train stations

## #fc What criteria do we grade a Computer Network on?
- Performance
	- Measured in Throughput and Delay
- Reliability
	- Measured in:
		- Accuracy of Deliver
		- Failure Rate
		- Recovery Time
		- Robustness
- Security
	- Protections from unauthorized access/damage
