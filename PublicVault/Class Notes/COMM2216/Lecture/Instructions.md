# Terminology
- Instruction
	- The sequence of steps to perform a task
- Procedure
	- The whole written task or series of separate instruction sets that make up a complex task
- Manual
	- Multiple procedures associated with an app or mechanism
# Purpose


# What do we know about the user?
- Will not read the full instructions before starting
- Will scan for whatever looks best/jumps out
- Will not remember information from step-to-step
- Follow the cardinal rule:
	- Strictly chronological order, and spoon-feed information as it becomes relevant

# Structure of Procedures
## Introduction
- How does the procedure fir into the larger context?
- What is the benefit of performing the procedure?
- When or under what circumstances be followed, and by whom?
- What tools and materials will be required?
- How much time will it take?
- Notes, cautions, warning that apply to the procedure as a whole?
- Typographic conventions
## Step-by-step Instructions
- What specific action should the reader perform, and in what sequence?
- What warnings, cautions, warning, and danger alerts apply to these individual step
	- Must appear before the step, and should not be numbered
- Number all steps
- Begin every step in the Imperative mood
	- "17. Attach the hose to the drain valve"
	- Make sure the verb describes the action that drives the process
		- Not "Go to the top right corner and click start"
		- instead: "Click start in the top right corner"
	- You can start with an adverb if it is important how the action is performed
		- "Carefully..."
		- "Calmly and slowly..."
	- Do not have the word "Step" before the step number.
	- You can use : or . or ), so long as you are consistent.
- Include graphics wherever you can.
- Subdivide long instructions into sections
	- To change a tire:
		- "Removing the tire" 
		- "Putting a new tire on"
### Notes, Cautions, Warnings, Danger
- Place immediately before the step to which they apply
- They are there to prevent people from making mistakes
- Notes make steps easier
- Cautions prevent failure of procedure or damage to equipment
- Warnings prevent injury
- Danger prevent death
## Conclusions
- What test will let the user know they have completed the steps correctly?
- Should be quick
	- "All three status lights should be a steady green"
- Add a troubleshooting step if there is the possibility of an error
	- "If one of the status lights is flashing yellow..."

## Troubleshooting
- Describe how to resolve predictable and common error in following the procedure
