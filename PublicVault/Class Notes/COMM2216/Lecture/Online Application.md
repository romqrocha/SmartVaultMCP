# Tweaking online submissions
- Software checks for specific terms both in the cover letter and resume 
	- You can paste the job description into a word-cloud-generator(wordcloud.com) to see what terms are most emphasized
	- Use those words into your resume, etc.
	- Paste the resume into a tester (jobscan.co)
- Software is not good at reading 
	- headers, 
	- tables, 
	- abbreviations, 
	- cursive fonts, and 
	- graphics
- Consider creating 2 resumes
	1) A formatted one for human readers
	2) An un-formatted one for ATS
	- This applies to application letters as well
