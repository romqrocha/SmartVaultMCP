- Resumes are the most important document you'll ever write
- Recruiters receive, on average, 250 CV's per job
- The first resume is sent in ~200 seconds after the job is posted
- On average, 5-7 seconds is spent looking at each resume
	- The goal isn't to find who to HIRE but to find who to exclude
	- A single spelling/grammar mistake may cause your resume to be thrown out
- 76% of resumes are ignored if the email is 'unprofessional'
- ~1 in 6 cover letters actually get read
- 1 in 3 employers have dismissed a candidate for something found on social media
## Resume vs CV
- Resume is short, 1-2 pages, and summarizes
- CV is your whole life
