# Preparing to write
- Ask yourself: 
	- What are my strengths and weaknesses?
	- What do I have to offer?
	- What can I say to show that i am
		- Reliable
		- Responsible

# Organizing
- 3 kinds
1) Standard
	- When education is the best thing you have going for you
2) Functional
3) Focused
## Breakdown
- Title block
	- Your name needs to be the biggest thing on the page and at the top
- Contact info
- Career Objective (Optional)
- Skills Summary
- Related Experience
	- Projects can go here
- *Education*
	- Used only if you have nothing else to say, experience trumps this.
- Other Experience
- Honors/Activities & Interests/Memberships/Special Skills
- References (Optional)
	- Really, if you have space
# Formatting
- Put the most important information/section first
- Use reverse chronological order
- Most important information under jobs/education/projects is not dates, its the *title* and *location*
- Profile should read like a skills summary
- Dates are unimportant, should be in the margin
# Wording
- Focus on activities and accomplishments that showcase
	- leadership
	- initiative
	- teamwork
	- resilience
- Use strong adverbs and adjectives
	- Action (What)
		- ->Task (Method or purpose, how and why)
		- ->Result (Accomplishment)
	- Accomplished [x] as measured by [y] by doing [z]
	- Start with result, then explain how you did it
- Your cover letter/resume have to assure the employer that your meet their requirements
