## Terminology

- (in)tangible business benefits
    - Anything easily quantifiable as NUMBERS
        - Market share may SEEM tangible, because the dollar value isn’t immediately calculatable.
            - Counter part would be **increased sales**, since there’s a dollar value attached.
- Questionnaire
    - wide-reaching, useful for outside the business in particular. Other options are better for within the business.
- Functional/non-functional requirements
    - _**pro-tip: Use process of elimination on non-functional requirements as they are easy to identify and define**_
    - Functional:
        - Information-oriented
        - Process-oriented
    - Non-Functional:
        - Operational
        - Performance
        - Security
        - Cultural & Political
- Context Diagram
    - just the name of the system and each entity. NO DATA STORES.
- Level 0 diagram
    - All core functions, data stores, and entities included in Context Diagram
