Oral Presentation

- 5 marks, 10 categories
    - Opening
        - Confident opening; demands the audiences attention
        - Ends with an outline slide
        - Introduces the speakers
    - Logical organization
        - Clear structure
        - Well supported points
        - 6 minutes =/- 30 seconds.
    - Eye contact
    - Good delivery
        - Confident voice
        - good enunciation
        - natural pace and tone
    - Good group dynamics
        - Clear transitions and introductions
    - No distracting mannerisms
    - Good overheads
        - Slides not cramped
        - Appropriate lettering size
        - Appropriate graphics
        - generally nice design
    - Proper use of overheads
        - Introducing topics
        - pausing to allow audience to comprehend the slides
        - use of bullets to keep pace
    - Proper summary at the end
        - Reprise the main points
        - Don’t just repeat the outline
        - **we’ll be doing the retrospective instead of the summary**
    - Answered questions well and fully. Confirmed the questions before answering.
