- Subject Line: Communicates the main idea
- Main idea: Clearly identify what you want to happen/what you want them to know by the end of the email.
- Context: Everything that led up to you writing the email
    - Can think of it as “Background”
    - Helps the reader understand **why** they should care about this email and what led you to the decision.
    - “Before I solve your problem, let me explain how I came to the solution/what the solution is”
- Next Steps:
    - What you want the reader to do, now that they have the full story.

### ChatGPT warning

- Almost always too verbose
- Includes all nouns in a given scenario
- Adds technical details that were likely not in the given scenario.
- Always starts with “I hope this email finds you well”, which you **really** shouldn’t do. Pointless pleasantries’ aren’t needed.
