- Header at the start

Introduction:

- To introduce the purpose
- how did it come to exist
- the methodology
- a brief outline

Summaries:

- Brings together the conclusions
- Must be complete and concise; no length limit
- Should be nothing new, nothing that’s not in the body of the report
- Think of it as a second version designed for the readers convenience; should ~10% of the body, excluding recommendations and conclusions

Figures:

- Must have a description in preceding text
- Must have a caption and source
