# Placement Calculation

Placement calculation done in order of:

1. Option Preference
2. Term 1 GPA
3. Priority

Priority calculations:

1. CST Level 2 students who completed all year 1 courses by the start of term 3 & Returning Co-op students
2. Students who completed Year 1 courses in more then 2 terms & students who had to go on a modified plan
3. Students who want to restart their CST studies
4. Students who failed or withdrew from their previous option

## What this means

- Deferring a term is **not** allowed
- This means you need to drop from the program and come back in, which assumes there is a seat for you
- That puts you in priority 3, and hurts your chances of getting the option you want, **even if** the option is held.
