# What are flex courses

- “Flex” is part-time studies
- Most classes are evenings and weekends
- Designed for self-driven students
    - No separate lectures/labs
    - More independent learning
    - Can be online, BBY, or DT

# Do we need them

- No.
- It is more expensive, and consumes your break.
- It does not speed up graduation

# Why do FT students take them

- Reduce future term workloads
- Make up failed/withdrawn courses
- On a modified plan

# Considerations and impacts

- Flex courses are **Equivalent** courses, but not **the same** course
    - SQL FT = Comp 1600
- FT take flex over the break, from April to August
- Overlaps the 5 week project term
- Only weekend and evening flex courses area allows to avoid schedule clashes
- Flex courses have ~10-15 hour/week load, including class time.
- Only 1 flex course can be taken concurrently with the 5 week term
- Can enroll in a 2nd only if it starts in the last week of May
- You do **not** get a discount on future terms tuition for completing flex courses.
    - CST FT students pay for 12.5 credits per term, but we take 25-30 per term. Taking 1-2 flex courses doesn’t cause your tuition to reduce.
