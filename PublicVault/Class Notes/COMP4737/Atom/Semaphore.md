- A type of [[Mutual Exclusion]]
- Introduced by [[Dijkstra]], inspired by Train Semaphores that signal is a track is free
- Two [[Atomic Operations]]; $P$ and $V$, which are Down and Up respectively
- It is guaranteed that once a Semaphore Operation has started, no other process can access the Semaphore until the operation has either complete or been blocked.
- Two types; [[#Binary Semaphore]] and [[#Counting Semaphore]]
